What is FIC?

A finance club, built by students who wished one existed sooner.

Future Investors Collective is a nonprofit network of student-led investing clubs. We give you a ready-to-run curriculum, a community of other young investors, and a stage — an inter-school stock pitch competition — to show what you've learned. Your zip code shouldn't decide whether you learn to invest, so we built something any school can pick up and run with.

Why chapters love FIC

Everything you need to start a club that's actually worth showing up to.

Real Curriculum

Six lessons that take you from "what's a stock?" to building a real pitch — written for beginners, by students who started there too.

Live Stock Pitch Competition

Research a company, build an investment thesis, and present it live against students from other schools.

Cross-School Network

Leadership That Looks Great on Apps

How it works

Three steps from "never opened a stock chart" to "pitched it on stage."

1

Learn the curriculum

Work through six beginner-friendly lessons covering the market, companies, financial statements, and how to value a stock.

2

Run an in-school pitch

Put your chapter's knowledge to work. Teams research a real company and build their first investment pitch together.

3

Compete across schools

Your top pitches go head-to-head with other FIC chapters at our inter-school stock pitch competition each spring.
